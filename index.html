Honest answers, no hype.

What is $CAPY?

A community memecoin on Solana themed around the capybara, the calmest animal alive. It is a fun, low-stress token, not a business or a promise of returns.

Is the contract safe?

Liquidity is burned and the contract is renounced, so no one can mint new tokens, change the tax or drain the pool. Always do your own research before buying anything.

What is the tax?

Zero on both buys and sells. There is no reflection mechanic and no team fee taken from trades.

Why yuzu?

Since 1982 the capybaras at Izu Shaboten Zoo in Japan get a warm open-air bath every winter, and keepers float yuzu citrus in it. The photos became a meme. We just made a coin about it.

Will this make me rich?

Probably not, and anyone who promises otherwise is lying. $CAPY is a meme, not an investment. Only spend what you are completely fine losing, and stay capy about it.

Ready to slow down?

Grab a bag, close the laptop and let the herd do the worrying. Which is to say: none.
